## Two caveats, stated so the number is not over-read

1. **Arrival rate.** The traffic in this log was generated by `simulate_traffic.py` with a 7 s pause between requests, so "today's peak minute" (8 requests) is the simulator's pacing. The conclusion does not depend on it: the key's ceiling is **~8 queries/minute** (8,000 TPM ÷ ~1,000 tokens), so any real traffic averaging more than 0.8 queries/minute today hits the TPM cap at 10x, long before cost or the 1,000/day request cap.
2. **The fix makes it tighter.** Measured on the same log, sliced by prompt version: `insurance-qa-v2` = 998 tokens/query, $0.000230/query (62 requests); `insurance-qa-v4` = 1,413 tokens/query, $0.000335/query (5 canary requests). At v4 the ceiling drops to 8,000 ÷ 1,413 ≈ **5.7 queries/minute**. Cost per day at 10x stays below $0.10 either way, so the rate limit is still what breaks first.
